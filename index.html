FROM LIGHT TO A SIGNAL

What happens
between the frames.

A transparent signal-processing baseline. No trained model or cloud service required.

01 / SAMPLE

Find a patch of skin

Average the red, green and blue pixels in your selected region for each new video frame.

02 / SEPARATE

Bring out the variation

Normalize colour channels and apply the POS projection in overlapping 1.6-second windows.

03 / ESTIMATE

Look for a stable rhythm

Use a windowed FFT to find a peak, then check timing, exposure and consistency before showing BPM.

RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

Not clinically validated. Motion, lighting, compression and skin reflectance can affect results. Signal checks are heuristics, not an accuracy score. Do not use this for diagnosis or health decisions. The region is selected manually; this version does not track faces.
